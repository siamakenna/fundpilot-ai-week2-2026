## Development sources

### CNS 2027 Travel Awards — current application round

[Official source](https://www.cogneurosociety.org/travel-award/).
Applications opened October 5 and close November 3, 2026; decisions are scheduled
for December 4. Current paid student/postdoc membership, first authorship and
presenting the submitted poster are required. The applicant must submit an abstract,
statement and advisor support; in-person presentation is required. Withdrawal
forfeits the prize; recipients cannot receive it in the following year. The $500
is a sponsor stipend, not an applicant request cap. Selection is competitive.

Short excerpt: “Applicants must be the first author and presenter of a poster submitted for presentation at the CNS 2027 Annual Meeting.”

### ISMB 2026 Conference Fellowships — historical application cycle

[Official source](https://www.iscb.org/ismb2026/general-info/conference-fellowships).
The page gives June 5 notification and June 11, 2026 registration deadlines;
application dates are embedded separately, so no exact application cutoff is
invented. Invitation and accepted work, paid membership through year-end, presenting
authorship and attendance matter. Late-breaking posters are excluded. The text
mentions postdoctoral applicants broadly but says current federal funding excludes
postdocs and US federal employees: this override must not be lost. Applicants must
advance expenses. Geography controls award maxima; a meal-expense ceiling is
separate from an applicant request limit. Waitlisted funding is conditional.

Short excerpt: “Applicant must be able to pay all expenses of attending the conference up front”

### SfN TPDA 2026 — closed, historical test

[Official source](https://www.sfn.org/tpda).
Applications closed June 30, 2026 at 5 PM Eastern. Presenting authorship, membership
and eligible trainee status are required; a near-future educational start is an
exception to current enrollment. Only one application per abstract is allowed.
Another specified SfN travel award in the same/prior calendar year excludes the
nominee. Materials have length limits and completeness rules. Location-dependent
$1,000/$2,500 stipends are benefits; virtual recipients get registration without a
travel stipend. In-person recipients have poster/workshop obligations. Optional
science-writing consideration has additional later duties.

Short excerpt: “Applicant must be a current member of SfN.”

### Company of Biologists Travelling Fellowships — current round

[Official source](https://www.biologists.com/grants/travelling-fellowships/).
Next deadline October 23, 2026, for travel after December 7. Up to £3,000 supports
collaborative laboratory visits by graduate students/postdocs. Journal relevance,
one-person/lab/journal/round limits, travel timing, supporting letters and English
materials apply. Training courses and retrospective requests are excluded. Awards
are limited to once per two years; sanctions and permitted-expense restrictions
apply. Nationality is generally unrestricted. Some conditions concern use and
reporting of funds after award rather than initial eligibility.

Short excerpt: “We do not provide funds to attend training courses”

### Biochemical Society General Travel Grants — current round

[Official source](https://www.biochemistry.org/grants-and-awards/grants-and-bursaries/general-travel-grants/).
Next stated round closes November 2, 2026 for events after December 1. Membership
normally needs 12 months, with a six-month exception for multiyear members. An
abstract and eligible attendance expenses are needed. Consecutive-year awards and
FEBS Congress/Young Scientists’ Forum applications are excluded. £500/£1,000 are
award tiers; £2,000 over three years is an aggregate funding restriction, not the
same as a single-request ceiling. Financial need and career benefit are assessed
competitively; some applicant categories need a support letter. Retrospective,
late and incomplete applications are not accepted.

Short excerpt: “Applicants cannot receive funding from the same scheme in consecutive years.”

## Limits of the evidence

A page can change after retrieval. Date information is a snapshot, not live opening
status. Read the original and linked policies before applying. The evaluation uses
eight selected passages per source, not a claim that all conditions across a whole
site were extracted. Annotations are assistant-reviewed and require independent
human review before treating them as authoritative reference labels.
